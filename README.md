# v0.0.1 release of Orokinex

i want to introduce the concept behind orokinex, explain what the app does, how it differs from other warframe companion tools, and most importantly, how it can help you reach your mastery rank goal.


## what is orokinex?

i'm a solo developer building a free fan-made tool for warframe. orokinex is a standalone mastery rank tracker that lets you mark items you've mastered and content you've completed. based on your progress and personal preferences, it suggests items and content you haven't mastered yet, helping you figure out what to work on next.

you can manually set your current mastery rank, track your remaining mastery xp, and define your target rank. you can also mark items as mastered, planned, in progress, or not planned, depending on your preferences and goals.

orokinex does not read any game, inventory, or account data and never interacts with the warframe client. all progress is tracked manually in a standalone application that runs independently of warframe and overwolf.


## what makes orokinex different?

the goal is to make mastery rank progression easier to understand and plan. instead of keeping track of everything yourself, you can use the app to organize your remaining goals and decide what to work on next.

you can filter items by category, choose which types of content you want to focus on, and account for prime and vaulted items. grind ratings help you estimate how much effort certain items may require, based on my own experience and research. you can also adjust these ratings to suit your own preferences.

an interactive pie chart gives you an overview of your missing mastery xp, helping you understand where your remaining progress comes from.

## what's next?

orokinex is still in its early stages, and there's plenty of room for improvement. i'm planning to expand the suggestions for weapons and companions, improve the way unavailable items are displayed, and add more customization options.

i'd also like to build a community where users can suggest and vote on future features, helping shape the direction of the project.
